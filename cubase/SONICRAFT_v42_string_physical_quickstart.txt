SONICRAFT v4.2 — Cubase String Physical Quickstart

1. Drag MusicXML/XML/MXL onto COMPILE_MUSICXML_STRINGS_v42.bat.
2. Import the generated MIDI.
3. Keep Q4 Multi layout; legacy CH1–4 plus extra 4x4 voice lanes are written automatically.
4. Edit per-note performance in MIDI CC lanes:
   CC21 Stack, 22 Dynamics, 23 Vibrato, 24 Transition, 25 Attack, 26 Tightness
   CC27 String, 28 Position, 29 Bow Direction, 30 Bow Change, 31 Bow Pressure
   CC33 Contact Point, 34 Portamento Route, 35 Divisi Desk
5. Do not use CC32 for SONICRAFT physical data; it is Bank Select LSB.
